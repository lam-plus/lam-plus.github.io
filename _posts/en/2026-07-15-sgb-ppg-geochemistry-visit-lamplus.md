	<p>On July 15, 2026, LAM+ welcomed researchers from the Geological Survey of Brazil (SGB) and graduate students from the Graduate Program in Geosciences (Geochemistry) at Fluminense Federal University for a technical visit to the laboratory facilities.</p>

	<p>The meeting featured an intensive exchange of experience and a productive brainstorming session. Key topics included rare earth elements (REE), the development and improvement of analytical protocols, and opportunities to integrate different techniques for material identification, characterization, and mapping.</p>

	<p>New applications involving hyperspectral imaging, X-ray fluorescence, electron microscopy, and artificial intelligence tools were also discussed. The conversations helped identify ideas, innovative approaches, and shared interests that may lead to collaborative projects and activities.</p>

	<p>The interaction among researchers, students, and institutions strengthens the development of new partnerships and expands opportunities for scientific and technological progress in geosciences and related fields.</p>
