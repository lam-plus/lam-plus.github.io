	<h1>Publications</h1>

	<p>LAM+ brings together researchers with a consolidated scientific record in geosciences, oceanography, paleoceanography, geochemistry, imaging, analytical instrumentation, and artificial intelligence applied to sedimentary sequences.</p>

	<p>As the new laboratory moves through commissioning and assisted operation, this section is being prepared to host publications directly associated with LAM+ activities, including scientific papers, technical protocols, data products, reports, book chapters, conference communications, and outputs derived from analyses performed in the laboratory.</p>

	<p>The previous scientific production of associated researchers can be found in their academic profiles, including ORCID, Lattes, Google Scholar, and institutional pages.</p>

	<p>This page will be updated soon with the first publications linked to LAM+.</p>

	<p>We are building the technical and scientific foundation for a new generation of high-resolution studies of sedimentary sequences.</p>
