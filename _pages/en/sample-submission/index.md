  <h1>Sample Submission</h1>

  <div class="submission-lead">
    <div class="submission-lead-media">
      <img src="{{ '/assets/img/hero/sample-preparation.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Sample preparation" />
    </div>
    <div class="submission-lead-text">
      <p>
        LAM+ accepts requests for technical screening of sedimentary, geological, and associated materials.
        Analyses may involve imaging, scanning, compositional characterisation, curation, and integration with artificial intelligence.
      </p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="submission-notice">
    <strong>Please note:</strong> Submission of the form does not imply automatic acceptance of the sample or immediate scheduling of the analysis.
    All requests are subject to technical screening by the LAM+ team.
  </div>

  <h2>Before submitting</h2>
  <p>Please have the following information ready before filling in the form:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Original sample identification;</li>
    <li>Project, institution, or source collection;</li>
    <li>Material type and physical condition (dry, wet, consolidated, unconsolidated);</li>
    <li>Approximate quantity, dimensions, or volume;</li>
    <li>Requested analysis or analyses;</li>
    <li>Name and contact of the responsible person;</li>
    <li>Notes on preservation, handling, contamination, or usage restrictions.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Sample types</h2>
  <p>LAM+ may evaluate, among other materials:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Whole or sectioned sediment cores (open or closed);</li>
    <li>Raw or prepared discrete sediment samples;</li>
    <li>Other geological materials;</li>
    <li>Or other materials that may be assessed according to technical feasibility.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>Acceptance of each material depends on prior technical screening.</p>

  <h2>Possible analytical workflows</h2>
  <p>Samples may be assessed for one or more of the following analytical routes, subject to technical feasibility:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>VNIR or SWIR hyperspectral imaging (or both);</li>
    <li>RGB/CIELab colour imaging;</li>
    <li>Photographic record and curation;</li>
    <li>High-resolution XRF core scanning;</li>
    <li>Benchtop or portable XRF;</li>
    <li>SEM-EDS;</li>
    <li>Data processing and artificial intelligence integration.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Preparation and identification</h2>
  <p>Samples must:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Be clearly labelled and properly packaged;</li>
    <li>Preserve spatial orientation where applicable;</li>
    <li>Be accompanied by minimum provenance information;</li>
    <li>Include prior notice of any biosafety, contamination, or disposal restrictions.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>
    For sediment cores, please provide when possible: length, diameter, condition (open/closed),
    top-to-base orientation, and history of prior analyses or preparation.
  </p>

  <h2>What happens after registration</h2>
  <p>
    The LAM+ team will carry out a preliminary screening and may contact you to request additional information,
    discuss sample preparation, assess technical feasibility, define the analytical workflow, and arrange
    physical delivery of the sample, where applicable.
  </p>
